Women Skill Development

Comprehensive vocational tailoring, pattern drafting, and garment construction training empowering rural women across Karatagi and Yaradona centres in Koppal District.

📸 View 20+ Photos Learn More
State Skill Initiative

Koushalya Karnataka Yojane (CMKKY)

Under the flagship Koushalya Karnataka initiative of the Skill Development, Entrepreneurship and Livelihood Department (SDEL), Government of Karnataka, PCDF Trust facilitates intensive vocational training to bridge rural youth with self-employment and sustainable livelihood.

Running concurrently at Karatagi and Yaradona (Koppal District), the programme equips rural women and aspiring young tailors with practical, market-aligned skills in garment construction, drafting, machine maintenance, and apparel business setup — completely free of cost.

Key Pillars

What Trainees Learn

A hands-on, rigorous curriculum transitioning beginners into confident garment entrepreneurs.

✂️

Pattern Drafting & Layout

Mastering precision paper patterns, standard body measurements, grainline alignment, and fabric-efficient cutting layouts.

⚙️

Industrial Sewing Machine

Complete operation, treadle coordination, needle threading, tension adjustment, bobbin winding, and routine preventive maintenance.

👗

Garment Assembly

Stitching kurtis, blouses, skirts, traditional wear, collars, zippers, buttonholing, and fine finishing to commercial boutique standards.

📐

Measurement & Fitting

Accurate client measurement taking, fitting adjustments, fault detection, alteration techniques, and quality inspection.

💼

Entrepreneurship & Costing

Pricing garments, sourcing raw fabrics, customer handling, boutique management, and connecting with local village markets.

🏅

Certification & Livelihood

Formal skill assessment and recognized credentials opening avenues for micro-enterprises, SHG ventures, and garment unit jobs.
