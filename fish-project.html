🐟 NFDB SPONSORED PROJECT

Fish Farming Training &
Capacity Building

Empowering SC/ST fisher communities in Kanvithimmalapur, Karnataka through modern aquaculture training, certification, and livelihood support.

Programme Overview

National Fisheries Development Board (NFDB) Training

This project focuses on the capacity building of fisher communities belonging to SC/ST categories under the Schedule Caste Sub Plan (SCSP) & Tribal Sub Plan (TSP).

Organised by the Agriculture Skill Council of India (ASCI) and sponsored by the National Fisheries Development Board (NFDB), this intensive training programme is facilitated on the ground by PCDF Trust at Kanvithimmalapur. It aims to introduce modern fish farming techniques, improve productivity, and secure sustainable livelihoods for marginalised rural families.
