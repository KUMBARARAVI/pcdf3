Who We Are

PCDF Trust — Building Communities from the Ground Up

Progressive Community & Development Foundation (PCDF) Trust is a Karnataka-based non-profit organisation committed to improving rural livelihoods through evidence-based community programmes. We work directly at the grassroots — engaging farmers, women, and local communities to create lasting, measurable change.

Our programmes span sustainable agriculture, womens vocational training, and community capacity building — all aligned with India national development priorities and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

Our Work

Active Projects

Three flagship initiatives running simultaneously across Ballari District, Karnataka — each making a measurable difference in the lives of rural communities.

🌾 Agriculture
AWD — Alternate Wetting & Drying Project

A sustainable paddy irrigation initiative that trains farmers to use the Alternate Wetting & Drying (AWD) technique — saving up to 30% water without reducing crop yield. Covers 12 villages across Ballari & Koppal Districts.

🧵 Vocational Training
PM Samarth — Sewing & Tailor Training

A Government of India-backed vocational programme under the PM Samarth Scheme (Ministry of Textiles) that provides free sewing machine and tailoring training to rural women in Muddapura and Kampli, Ballari District.

Our Journey

Project Milestones

Key events across both PCDF Trust projects in 2026.

Jan/Feb 2026

NFDB Fish Farming Training

Capacity building program for SC/ST fisher communities begins at Kanvithimmalapur. Trainees verified and ASCI certified.

February 2026

PM Samarth — Batch 1 Launched

First batch of women enrolled at Muddapura No.10 Training Centre. 20+ women start tailoring training.

March 2026

AWD Project — Field Season Begins

AWD tubes installed across 12 villages. Farmer training sessions and field monitoring activities commence for the season.

May 2026

PM Samarth — Certificate Distribution

Batch 1 graduates receive NSQF-recognised certificates at Kampli. Active garment production continues at Muddapura.

June 2026

AWD — Field Monitoring & Data

Continued field visits, tube readings, and harvest assessments. Impact data compiled across all 12 project villages.

Ongoing 2026

PM Samarth — New Batches Rolling

Multiple batches running at Muddapura and Kampli. 100+ women enrolled in total with placement support ongoing.
