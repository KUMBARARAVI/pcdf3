Who We Are

PCDF Trust — Building Communities from the Ground Up

Progressive Community & Development Foundation (PCDF) Trust is a Karnataka-based non-profit organisation committed to transforming rural lives through evidence-based, sustainable community development. We work directly at the grassroots — partnering with farmers, women, and youth to create lasting, measurable change.

Our five flagship initiatives span sustainable paddy agriculture, water conservation, women's apparel training, aquaculture, and state vocational skill programs — strictly aligned with national priorities and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

A flagship sustainable rice production initiative in partnership with Core CarbonX & Aurigraph Co — deploying AWD pani pipes to cut methane emissions, save 30–55% irrigation water, and build carbon market readiness across 24,708+ acres.

Our Journey

Project Milestones

Key achievements across PCDF Trust projects throughout 2026.

January — February 2026

NFDB Fish Farming Training

Capacity building program for SC/ST fisher communities launched at Kanvithimmalapur. Trainees verified and ASCI certified.

February 2026

PM Samarth — Batch 1 Launched

First batch of women enrolled at Muddapura No.10 Training Centre. 20+ women start tailoring training.

March 2026

AWD Project — Field Season Begins

AWD perforated tubes installed across 12 villages. Farmer training sessions and field monitoring activities commence for the season.

May 2026

PM Samarth — Certificate Distribution

Batch 1 graduates receive NSQF-recognised certificates at Kampli town panchayat office. Active garment production continues at Muddapura.

June 2026

AWD — Field Monitoring & Yield Data

Continued field visits, tube readings, and harvest assessments. Impact data compiled across all 12 project villages.

July 2026

KSDC — Vocational Skill Batches

State skill development sessions initiated for rural youth and women with hands-on practice and employment mentoring.

August — September 2026

Koushalya Karnataka — Tailoring Program

Intensive sewing machine, pattern drafting, and garment construction batches at Karatagi & Yaradona. Completed with batch exhibition & celebrations.
